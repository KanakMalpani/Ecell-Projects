CORPORATE ACCEPTABLE USE AND REMOTE WORK POLICY
Document ID: POL-HR-RW-2024 | Version 1.2

REMOTE WORK ELIGIBILITY
Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and HR documentation.

HOME OFFICE REQUIREMENTS
- Dedicated workspace with reliable internet (minimum 25 Mbps download)
- Ergonomic chair and desk setup (expense reimbursement up to $500 annually)
- Corporate VPN must be active during all work hours
- Webcam required for video meetings

EQUIPMENT POLICY
Company-provided laptops remain company property. Employees must not install personal software that conflicts with MDM policies. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 2 hours to IT and Security.

WORKING HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
Core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM in employee's local timezone. Employees must respond to Slack messages within 2 hours during business hours.

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Eligible expenses: internet stipend ($50/month), co-working space (pre-approved, max $300/month), office supplies. Submit via expense portal within 30 days with receipts.

DATA PROTECTION FOR REMOTE WORK
Confidential and Restricted data must not be stored on personal devices or cloud services. Use only approved tools: Google Drive (corporate), Confluence, and encrypted USB drives (IT-issued only).

PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS
Remote employees are evaluated on the same criteria as in-office staff. Managers conduct monthly 1:1 check-ins and quarterly performance reviews.

POLICY VIOLATIONS
Working from unauthorized locations (public cafes without privacy screen), sharing screen with unauthorized persons during confidential calls, or disabling VPN during work hours constitutes a policy violation.
