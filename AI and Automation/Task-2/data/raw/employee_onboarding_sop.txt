STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING
SOP-HR-ONB-003 | Version 1.4 | Owner: Human Resources

SCOPE
This SOP covers the end-to-end onboarding process for full-time employees joining the organization.

PRE-ARRIVAL (T-5 Business Days)
1. HR creates employee record in the HRIS system and assigns employee ID.
2. IT provisions laptop, email account, and badge access based on role template.
3. Hiring manager submits equipment and software access request form (Form IT-102).

DAY 1 CHECKLIST
- 09:00: Welcome session with HR representative in Conference Room B
- 09:30: IT setup session - laptop configuration, MFA enrollment, VPN installation
- 11:00: Security awareness training (mandatory, 45 minutes)
- 13:00: Department orientation with hiring manager
- 15:00: Benefits enrollment deadline briefing

ACCESS PROVISIONING
Standard employees receive: email, Slack, Google Workspace, Confluence (read), Jira (project-specific).
Managers additionally receive: expense approval portal, performance review system access.
Engineers additionally receive: GitHub organization membership, AWS sandbox account (dev only).

COMPLIANCE TRAINING
All new hires must complete within 5 business days:
- Information Security Fundamentals (ISF-101)
- Data Privacy and GDPR Awareness (DPG-201)
- Code of Conduct Acknowledgment (signed electronically)

OFFBOARDING REFERENCE
When an employee departs, IT must revoke all access within 4 hours of termination notification. See SOP-HR-OFF-002 for full offboarding procedure.

ESCALATION
Onboarding issues should be escalated to onboarding@company.com. SLA for access provisioning: 24 hours for standard roles, 48 hours for custom access requests.
