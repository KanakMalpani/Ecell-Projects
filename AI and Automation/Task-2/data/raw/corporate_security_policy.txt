ENTERPRISE IT SECURITY POLICY FRAMEWORK
Document ID: POL-SEC-2024-001 | Version 2.1 | Effective: January 2024

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes mandatory security controls for all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors accessing organizational information systems.

2. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
All user accounts must use passwords with a minimum length of 14 characters. Passwords must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all remote access and privileged accounts.

3. DATA CLASSIFICATION
Organizational data is classified into four tiers:
- Public: Information approved for external release
- Internal: Standard business data for employee use only
- Confidential: Customer data, financial records, and HR information
- Restricted: Trade secrets, encryption keys, and regulated health data (PHI)

4. INCIDENT REPORTING
Security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 1 hour of discovery via the incident portal or hotline ext. 9911. Critical incidents (data breach, ransomware) require immediate escalation to the CISO and legal counsel.

5. REMOTE ACCESS
Remote access is permitted only through the corporate VPN using certificate-based authentication. Split tunneling is prohibited. Personal devices may not access internal systems unless enrolled in Mobile Device Management (MDM).

6. ACCEPTABLE USE
Employees may not install unauthorized software, share credentials, or use corporate systems for cryptocurrency mining. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

7. POLICY REVIEW
This policy is reviewed annually by the Information Security Committee. Last review date: December 2023. Next review: December 2024.
